Life with Dixie

From an eight-week-old puppy to my lifelong companion

Meet Dixie 🐾 — my Australian Shepherd, loyal companion, and occasional guest in my videos. Here’s how our adventure began.

DIXIE'S STORY · HOW IT ALL BEGAN

The Day Dixie Chose Me

Back when I was attending college in New York, I traveled to Maryland to spend Thanksgiving with my family. I was usually on my own in New York, and I told them I'd really like to have a dog.

My aunt found a little Australian Shepherd puppy online in Ohio, and my grandmother called to ask if we could bring her home. The day after Thanksgiving, my uncle and I set off on a four-hour road trip.

When we arrived, we were completely confused. Dixie was on an Amish farm, and we couldn't find the house. No street name, no address—nothing to guide us. After several calls and a lot of searching, we finally found the place.

And then something happened I'll never forget. The moment we got out of the car, this tiny eight-week-old puppy walked right up to me and sat at my feet.

I like to think she chose me before I ever got the chance to choose her.

I paid the elderly Amish woman, and my uncle and I drove all the way back to Maryland that same day. Four hours there, four hours back—eight hours on the road for one little puppy.

That puppy was Dixie. I went to Ohio looking for a dog, and I came home with a best friend.

Eight hours on the road to bring her home. Years of memories that made every mile worth it. 🐾

About & Connect

The man behind Silent Gentleman

About Colin

I'm a Deaf Texan, machinist, and graduate in Organizational Management & Leadership, where I earned summa cum laude honors with a 4.0 GPA. I also studied CNC machining, earned a Lean Six Sigma Yellow Belt, and became an Eagle Scout.

My work has taught me the value of precision, teamwork, clear communication, and accessible training. I've also completed several months of biblical counseling training focused on the Deaf community, as a trainee. Through Silent Gentleman, I share what I've learned about faith, character, leadership, and everyday life.

Old-fashioned values. A modern world. A life still being built.

I'm Colin. Welcome.

My name is Colin. I'm a Deaf Texan who grew up in El Paso in a family with military and business backgrounds. I was homeschooled, became an Eagle Scout, and learned early that character, responsibility, and keeping your word matter.

I once dreamed of serving in the military or emergency services. Being Deaf meant those paths weren't open to me, so I found another direction through machining. Along the way, I earned a bachelor's degree in Organizational Management and Leadership and kept working toward the future I want to build.

I hope to have a family of my own someday, grow a business, travel, and keep discovering what life has to teach me.

Why Silent Gentleman?

I've always admired the gentlemanly qualities often associated with the 1940s: kindness, class, humility, wisdom, integrity, and respect. I believe those values still have a place in today's world.

I started Silent Gentleman to share what I've learned, including the mistakes I've made. From finding opportunities and developing skills to getting through difficult seasons or discovering a good book, I hope my experiences help someone else along the way.

My Christian faith is part of who I am, but you'll find more than faith here. I enjoy history, books, humor, adventure, practical knowledge, and conversations that make people think.

A Little More About Me

My sign name comes from the word Bold. I'm usually quiet and guarded when I first meet someone, but I love making people laugh, teasing friends, having deep conversations, and heading out on adventures. I enjoy listening to people's stories, too. If someone needs help and I'm able to help, I believe in showing up.

I'm a gentleman in progress, not a perfect example. I still have plenty of goals to accomplish and lessons to learn.

What I'm Building

Today, I'm sharing videos in American Sign Language (ASL) with captions, personal reflections, humor, and book recommendations. In time, I hope to grow Silent Gentleman into a place for merchandise, educational courses, and practical resources that help people pursue their goals.

Whether you're here for wisdom, history, faith, humor, or simply encouragement, you're welcome.
