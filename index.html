About & Connect

The man behind The Silent Gentleman

Old-fashioned values. A modern world. A life still being built.

I'm Colin. Welcome.

My name is Colin. I'm a Deaf Texan who grew up in El Paso in a family with military and business backgrounds. I was homeschooled, became an Eagle Scout, and learned early that character, responsibility, and keeping your word matter.

I once dreamed of serving in the military or emergency services. Being Deaf meant those paths weren't open to me, so I found another direction through machining. Along the way, I earned a bachelor's degree in Organizational Management and Leadership and kept working toward the future I want to build.

I hope to have a family of my own someday, grow a business, travel, and keep discovering what life has to teach me.

Why The Silent Gentleman?

I've always admired the gentlemanly qualities often associated with the 1940s: kindness, class, humility, wisdom, integrity, and respect. I believe those values still have a place in today's world.

I started The Silent Gentleman to share what I've learned, including the mistakes I've made. From finding opportunities and developing skills to getting through difficult seasons or discovering a good book, I hope my experiences help someone else along the way.

My Christian faith is part of who I am, but you'll find more than faith here. I enjoy history, books, humor, adventure, practical knowledge, and conversations that make people think.

A Little More About Me

My sign name comes from the word Bold. I'm usually quiet and guarded when I first meet someone, but I love making people laugh, teasing friends, having deep conversations, and heading out on adventures. I enjoy listening to people's stories, too. If someone needs help and I'm able to help, I believe in showing up.

I'm a gentleman in progress, not a perfect example. I still have plenty of goals to accomplish and lessons to learn.

What I'm Building

Today, I'm sharing videos in American Sign Language (ASL) with captions, personal reflections, humor, and book recommendations. In time, I hope to grow The Silent Gentleman into a place for merchandise, educational courses, and practical resources that help people pursue their goals.

Whether you're here for wisdom, history, faith, humor, or simply encouragement, you're welcome.
